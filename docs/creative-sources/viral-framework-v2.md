   Create a gap between what the viewer EXPECTS to feel and what they ACTUALLY
   feel. Start with tension, discomfort, or a provocative claim — then resolve
   it into empowerment, relief, or pride. The emotional swing IS the virality.
   → Pattern: Negative setup → Surprising positive reframe → Elevated feeling
   → The bigger the swing, the more shareable the content
